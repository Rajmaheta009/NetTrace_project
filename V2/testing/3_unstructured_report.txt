Surveillance Report - Case File 042

On the evening of 14 March, Rakesh Verma was observed meeting Anita Rao at a
warehouse on the outskirts of Pune, registered to Vikram Shah. The meeting
lasted approximately forty minutes. Shortly before arriving, Verma placed a
call from +91-9876543210 to a number believed to belong to Anita Rao. A
delivery van bearing plate number MH-12-CD-5678 was seen parked at the
warehouse for the duration of the visit. Verma was previously linked to
Coastal Traders Pvt Ltd, and financial records suggest Anita Rao has acted
as an advisor for the same organization over the past six months.
